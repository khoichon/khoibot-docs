# khoibot's Games

Games are *very* important. Trust me.

## Decrypt

The bot will choose a word, and hide most of the letters. The job of the players is to guess the original word. Every 30 seconds, the bot will reveal another letter.

## Anagram

The bot will choose a word, and shuffle the letters, where the players will have to guess the original word.

## Numguess

The bot will choose a number from 1 to the set maximum number, and you will have to guess that number.

Every time you guess a number, the bot will tell you whether its number is higher, lower or is correct.
